Ghost button: opens only from the button, survives the gap

Move down into the menu: the 6 px gap is covered by an invisible bridge, and leaving starts a 250 ms grace, so a slightly loose path does not close it. It closes when the pointer is off both for 250 ms, on Escape, or after a pick. Click works too (touch and keyboard: Enter opens, arrows move).
